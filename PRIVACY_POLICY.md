# Privacy Policy for Margdarshan

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

**Margdarshan** ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Gita Brain, is a personal spiritual reflection and study tool based on the Bhagavad Gita. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal reflections remain yours.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Margdarshan mobile application.

---

### 1. Summary of Core Principles
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal identity details (name, email, phone number) to use Margdarshan.
- **Your Journals Stay on Your Device:** Your personal reflections, mood logs, and Japa meditation sessions are stored locally on your device.
- **No Ads or Tracking:** We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app analytics trackers.

---

### 2. Information Handled by the App

#### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
- **Daily Journal Entries & Mood Reflections:** Stored exclusively in local storage on your device. This data is never automatically uploaded to any remote database.
- **Japa Meditation Counts & History:** Stored locally on your device to track your meditative progress.
- **App Preferences:** Language selection, dark/light theme, and reminder timings are saved locally.

*Note: Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored journals unless you back them up.*

#### B. Guidance Queries Sent to the Gita Brain Service
- When you type or dictate a question or situation into the guidance search bar, your query text is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our backend service (Gita Brain) to retrieve relevant verses and generate a structured philosophical reflection.
- Your query is used strictly to produce your real-time response. We do not store queries tied to your personal identity, nor do we sell or monetize your queries.

#### C. Microphone Access
- **Permission (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** The app requests microphone access solely when you explicitly tap the microphone button to dictate a question.
- Voice input is converted to text using device-level speech recognition. Raw voice recordings are never stored or retained on our servers.

#### D. Notifications & Alarms
- **Permissions (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Used solely to deliver local reminders for Japa meditation and daily journaling at times you select in Settings. All alarms run locally on your device.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
To provide AI-assisted scriptural interpretation, your query text is processed via secure API calls. These third-party services process the text solely to generate responses and do not use your inputs to track you across different websites or apps.

The app contains no third-party advertising or commercial tracking libraries.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion
We respect your right to control your personal data:
- **Retention:** Since MaargDarshan does not require user accounts, all your personal entries (reflections, journal notes, mood logs, and meditation stats) are saved exclusively on your local device. We do not retain personal records on remote servers.
- **How to Delete Your Data:** You can completely and permanently delete all your stored data at any time by navigating to your Android device settings: `Settings > Apps > MaargDarshan > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data`, or by uninstalling the app.
- **Data Deletion Inquiries:** Because we do not store identifying user accounts on our servers, no server-side user data remains once you clear local storage. For any verification or privacy inquiries, contact us at `03rajpurohit@gmail.com`.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy
Margdarshan does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because the app requires no account creation and collects no personal identifiers, it is safe for general audiences interested in philosophical and spiritual study.

---

### 6. Not Medical or Mental Health Advice
Margdarshan is an educational and spiritual reflection tool grounded in the Bhagavad Gita. It is **not** a healthcare application and does **not** provide psychological, psychiatric, clinical, or medical advice. If you are experiencing emotional distress or a mental health crisis, please consult a qualified healthcare professional or emergency support services.
