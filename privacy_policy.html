Privacy Policy for Margdarshan

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Margdarshan ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Gita Brain, is a personal spiritual reflection and study tool based on the Bhagavad Gita. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal reflections remain yours.

1. Summary of Core Principles

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal identity details (name, email, phone number) to use Margdarshan.
  • Your Journals Stay on Your Device: Your personal reflections, mood logs, and Japa meditation sessions are stored locally on your device.
  • No Ads or Tracking: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app analytics trackers.

2. Information Handled by the App

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your daily reflections, mood tracking, and Japa counter sessions are stored locally in your device's private application storage. This data is never automatically uploaded to any remote database. Uninstalling the app or clearing application storage may permanently remove locally stored journals.

B. Guidance Queries Sent to the Gita Brain Service

When you type or speak a question to seek guidance, your message text is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our backend service (Gita Brain) solely to retrieve relevant Bhagavad Gita verses and generate an explanation. We do not store queries tied to your personal identity, nor do we sell or monetize your queries.

C. Microphone Access

The app requests microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) solely when you tap the microphone button to dictate a query. Voice input is processed locally on-device where supported. Raw audio recordings are never stored or retained on our servers.

D. Notifications & Reminders

Permissions for notifications and exact alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) are used exclusively to deliver local reminders for Japa meditation and daily journaling at the times you schedule in Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide AI-assisted scriptural interpretation, your query text is processed via secure API calls. These services process the text solely to generate responses and do not use your inputs to track you across different websites or apps. The app contains no third-party advertising or commercial tracking libraries.

4. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your data completely:

  • Retention: Since Margdarshan does not require user accounts, all your personal entries (reflections, journal notes, mood logs, and meditation stats) are saved exclusively on your local device. We do not retain personal records on remote servers.
  • How to Delete Your Data: You can completely and permanently delete all your stored data at any time by going to your Android device settings: Settings > Apps > MaargDarshan > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by simply uninstalling the app.
  • Data Deletion Requests: Because we do not store identifying user accounts on our servers, no server-side user data remains once you clear local storage. For any verification or privacy inquiries, you may contact us at 03rajpurohit@gmail.com.

5. Children’s Privacy

Margdarshan does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The app collects no personal identifiers and is safe for general audiences interested in philosophical and spiritual study.

6. Not Medical or Mental Health Advice

Margdarshan is an educational and spiritual reflection tool grounded in the Bhagavad Gita. It is not a healthcare application and does not provide psychological, psychiatric, clinical, or medical advice. If you are experiencing emotional distress or a mental health crisis, please consult a qualified healthcare professional or emergency support services.
