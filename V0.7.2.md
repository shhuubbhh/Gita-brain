# V0.7.2 — Situation routing fix

This patch fixes the V0.7.1 jealousy failure and tightens situation routing.

## Fixed
- Jealousy now includes the Gita-relevant bridge concepts `desire`, `anger`, and `mind`, allowing BG 2.62–2.63 to surface for jealousy/comparison situations.
- Added natural-language jealousy phrases such as “everyone else”, “have what I want”, “feel behind”, “got promoted”, and similar comparison language.
- Added semantic fallback from strong jealousy/comparison concept evidence to the `jealousy` situation.
- Added more specific career-comparison phrases so “comparing my career...” does not get swallowed by the broad `career` cue used by `career_confusion`.
- Situation phrase scoring now gives more weight to specific multi-word phrases than generic one-word matches.
- Expanded regression tests.
